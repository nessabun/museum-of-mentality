Letter 6 - 03/09/1945

Transcription

March 9, 1945

Richard,

You are no longer around to read my letters. Mary Anne has gone missing. There was blood by the back door. I have no recollection of last night. I don't want to be without you any longer.
.......
Please wait for me, My Love.
.....
My Richard. I love you.

Forever Yours,
Betty

[There is an unidentified red stain at the bottom of the page.]

Gallery

Description

A damaged photograph that was attached to the letter of what seems to be Richard and Betty. The upper half has been torn or burned to the point where the figures' faces are unable to be identified. The edges are stained in a brown-ish red tint. Written on the bottom right corner is "May 7, 1939".
