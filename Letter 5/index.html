Letter 5 - 03/06/1945

Transcription

March 6, 1945

Richie,

Is it true? A letter came in today. It wasn't from you. Is it true? You are dead? Dead? Impossible. That can't be true. No. It can't be. I ripped it to shreds. I refuse to believe it. You promised. You promised me! How could you lie to me?! Richard

Richard Richard

Richard you

lied to me!!

How could you!?

I can't wait any longer.

Betty
